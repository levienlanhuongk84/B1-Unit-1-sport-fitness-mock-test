Before you start

Enter your name as on the class list, choose the skills you want to take, and press Start. Each skill has its own clock; inside a skill, every part shows the time you should give it.

What to take

Tick a whole skill, or only the parts you want to practise. The clock of a skill is the sum of the parts you choose.

  • You can leave and come back: your answers and the clocks are kept on this device until you submit.
